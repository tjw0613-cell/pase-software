# Third-party data

The teaching dataset is derived from public GSE234933 data deposited by its
original investigators in NCBI GEO. It is not newly generated patient data.
Source files, checksums, selection rules and original identifiers are recorded
in the files packaged under `src/pase/data/` and exposed by the tutorial.

The MIT License applies to PASE source code. It does not transfer ownership of
or relicense the original study's data. Users should cite the original study
and GEO accession, respect source terms, and assess suitability for their use.
See [GSE234933](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE234933)
and [NCBI's GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html).

The bundled data are a small selected myeloid-cell example. They are suitable
for learning the API, not for reproducing full-cohort findings or estimating
the original cohort's cell-type proportions.
